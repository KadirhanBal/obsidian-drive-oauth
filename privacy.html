Privacy policy

This personal setup uses Google Drive to synchronize an Obsidian vault across the owner’s devices. The app requests Google Drive’s file-level permission. Air Sync uses that permission to read and update files it creates or that the owner explicitly opens with the app.

Vault files stay in the owner’s local Obsidian vaults and Google Drive. Obsidian stores the OAuth credentials on each device and exchanges and refreshes tokens directly with Google. This GitHub Pages site receives the temporary authorization code from Google and forwards it to Obsidian. The page contains no analytics or token storage. GitHub hosts the site and may log access as described in its privacy statement.

The owner can revoke access under Google Account connections and manage or delete the synchronized files directly in Google Drive.
